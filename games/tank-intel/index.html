How rarity is determined

Vehicle recognition is estimated from tracked-player counts (60%), lifetime battle counts (30%), and presence across historical rosters (10%). Counts combine NA and EU samples; they do not cover every player. Missing data uses an availability estimate, not zero popularity.

Maps, equipment, consumables, and awards use labelled availability or condition estimates. These are recognition proxies, not a survey of player answers. Rarity is relative to each prompt: four familiarity bands, plus exactly one Tank Ace. Exposure ties use alphabetical order so scoring stays consistent.

Answer sources and scope

Unofficial World of Tanks PC fan game. Current and historical vehicles, removed maps, and retired equipment count when they fit the prompt. Test-only vehicles and internal map placeholders are excluded. Recognition ratings are estimates based on observed popularity and availability; they are not surveyed answer frequencies.
